## Mechanism readout

International competition creates a toggleable but consequential workload and visibility layer: selection, travel, national-team meaning, club release, and relationship carryover can all affect careers. The underlying mechanism is already covered by PW-181–186, PW-243, PW-257, and OPEN-10, so these events are evidence/partial extensions rather than “new” duplicates.

## Bounded blind spots

Viewership and attendance are not interchangeable with durable player-market value. The lane records the distinction and does not extrapolate from one tournament to permanent globalization.
