## Mechanism readout

A stadium is a revenue, identity, tourism, and scheduling asset; a media-rights failure can force a league or club to change production and distribution midstream. This partially extends PW-141–144, PW-165, PW-247, and OPEN-02/13/20.

## Bounded blind spots

Attendance is affected by season, opponent, and reporting convention. Corporate annual reports are used for stated business structure, not as independent proof of causality or profitability.
