## Mechanism readout

The KBO 2nd Draft is a useful negative-and-positive case: a prior Future FA design did not create enough movement, so the league revived a protected-player redistribution mechanism with compensation and mandatory registration. CPBL rules demonstrate roster slots as a scarce, time-dependent foreign-player market. Both are partial extensions of PW-236–242, PW-256, and OPEN-07/08/09.

## Bounded blind spots

Korean and Chinese-language rulebooks can change by season and were sampled at official pages rather than translated wholesale. The event index records the season/date and avoids treating a current roster rule as timeless.
