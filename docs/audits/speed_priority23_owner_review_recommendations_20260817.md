## Why this review was superseded

This pass stated the correct construct—physical running ability from the first running step to about 90 ft, including initial acceleration, peak speed and speed maintenance—but its actual verdict decisions still relied too strongly on current NPB+ top-speed rank.

The defect was clearest in four recommendations:

- カリステ: `POWERPRO_TOO_HIGH_OR_STALE`
- 郡司裕也: `POWERPRO_TOO_LOW`
- 野間峻祥: `POWERPRO_TOO_HIGH_OR_STALE`
- 古賀優大: `POWERPRO_TOO_LOW`

For each, direct acceleration and/or complete first-to-90ft physical evidence was missing or temporally mismatched. Current peak speed alone was therefore insufficient for a strong whole-construct verdict.

The pass also treated several ratings as `POWERPRO_PLAUSIBLE` despite insufficient observation of the current acceleration/maintenance dimensions.
